Destiny Church Scholarship

📍 East London

About the Scholarship

The Destiny Church Scholarship provides financial support to eligible members of Destiny Church who are pursuing higher education.

The scholarship is available to both undergraduate and postgraduate students. Final eligibility requirements and funding decisions will be determined by the church.

Scholarship Information

Study Level Undergraduate & Postgraduate
Church Membership Required
Funding To be determined by the church
Application Status Applications subject to review

Eligibility

Applicants must be members of Destiny Church. The church will verify membership before an application can be considered.

  • Applicant must be a verified church member.
  • Applicant must be enrolled or accepted at a recognised institution.
  • Applicant may be required to provide academic records.
  • Applicant may be required to provide proof of registration.
  • Additional requirements may be specified by Destiny Church.

Documents That May Be Required

  • Academic record / transcript
  • Proof of registration
  • Certified identification
  • Proof of church membership
  • Other supporting documents requested by the church
Important:
Submitting an application does not guarantee that funding will be awarded. Destiny Church will review the applicant's academic information, supporting documents and church membership before making a decision.
